About The Film Archive

The Film Archive is a free collection of public domain films that play in your browser. Their copyright either ran out or was never renewed, so anyone can legally watch and share them.

What's here

Hundreds of films, mostly from the silent era and the early sound years. German Expressionism, silent comedy, serials, early horror, and a lot of smaller titles that never made it onto a streaming service.

Everything plays in the browser. Nothing to install, and you don't need an account to look around.

Where the films come from

  • Prints come from the Internet Archive and from this site's own storage. Some films have more than one transfer available and you can switch between them on the film's page. Scan quality varies a lot.
  • Titles, synopses, artwork and credits come from TMDB.

The public domain

A film enters the public domain when its copyright term runs out, or when the owner didn't file a renewal. Renewal was easy to miss in the mid twentieth century, which is why so many well known films are free today.

Copyright is territorial. This catalog follows US public domain status, so a film that is free to watch here might still be under copyright where you are.
